A build of MenuMeters for Macs where 2.1.6.1 crashes at launch because `/var/run/pppconfd` is missing: so far macOS 27.2, Sequoia 15.8.2 and a Sonoma beta ([yujitach/MenuMeters#325](https://github.com/yujitach/MenuMeters/issues/325)). It is Joe Moreno's 2.1.6.2 ([joemoreno/MenuMeters](https://github.com/joemoreno/MenuMeters)), signed and notarized, with Sparkle pointed at this fork's releases. If 2.1.6.1 still starts on your Mac, you don't need it.

- Alexander Botev's fix for the crash, from [yujitach/MenuMeters#325](https://github.com/yujitach/MenuMeters/issues/325#issuecomment-6178898754).
- A ppp interface, such as an L2TP VPN, is still counted, from the system's own traffic counters.
- Upstream's unreleased changes since 2.1.6.1 are included.
- The app checks this fork's releases for new builds and installs them itself, through Sparkle. Builds from upstream or from other forks are never offered.

**Requirements.** macOS 12 or later, Intel or Apple silicon. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings. It replaces an existing MenuMeters installation and keeps its settings, though macOS may ask again before MenuMeters can open Activity Monitor.

**Install.** Unzip, move MenuMeters.app to your Applications folder, and open it.
